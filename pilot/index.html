Pilot questions

What do we need to bring?

A team lead who knows the task, an admin OK on which tools Jin may connect to, and a short weekly check-in.

What does Jin read?

The email, calendar, chat, and files your team connects, used only to prepare work for that person. Your admin can exclude any folder, channel, label, or team.

Can we stop at any time?

Yes. Each employee can disconnect their account at any time.

Does my manager see what Jin finds for me?

No. What Jin finds goes to the employee. Jin does not score or rank people.
